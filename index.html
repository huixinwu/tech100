Welcome to the Introduction to Engineering and Technology OER Course!

This Open Educational Resource (OER) provides comprehensive guidance to engineering technology students, focusing on the essential skills required to construct and manage engineering technology projects. The material is organized into three modules:

  • Module 1 - Application of AI in the Field of Engineering Technology: Covering the history, philosophy, and methodology of engineering and technology professions in computer, electrical, and mechanical disciplines. It also presents student with information to help students in their freshman semester as engineering technology students to successful achieve their academic goals.
  • Module 2 - Basic Math and Technical Skills: Introducing and reviewing mathematical and analytical skills, Word processing, data analysis, graphical presentation along with experimentation and data interpretation and presentation techniques.
  • Module 3 - Engineering and Technology Ethics: Presenting engineering data and designs and addressing ethical and professional considerations.

Each module includes lecture materials, hands-on lab exercises, and projects, available in PDF and Word formats, with supplemental instructional videos. Lab activities are integrated to reinforce concepts, and projects are designed to challenge students to apply their knowledge to real-world scenarios, with clear instructions, grading rubrics, and examples provided.

Course Description

An introduction to the history, philosophy and methodology of engineering and technology related professions. The disciplines of computer, electrical and mechanical engineering and technology are introduced. Basic mathematical, graphical and analytic skills are developed as well as experimentation and data analysis techniques. The analysis and presentation of engineering data and designs, as well as ethical and professional considerations, are considered.

Learning Outcome

Upon successful completion of this bootcamp, students will be able to:
  • Explain Engineering Practice and Ethics: Describe the engineering profession, professional responsibilities, engineering ethics, and the requirements for professional licensure, including the role of AI in ethical engineering decision-making.
  • Analyze Experimental Data Using AI: Collect, interpret, and analyze data from laboratory exercises representing different engineering disciplines, using appropriate mathematical methods, data visualization techniques, and AI-assisted analytical tools to identify patterns, evaluate results, and draw evidence-based conclusions..
  • Apply Computer Literacy and Engineering Software Tools: Demonstrate proficiency in computer applications and engineering software tools, including AI-powered tools, to solve introductory engineering problems, analyze information, and develop technical solutions while critically evaluating AI-generated outputs for accuracy and reliability.
