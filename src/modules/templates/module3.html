Module 3: Introduction to Engineering Technology Practices and Applications

This module introduces students to the practices, responsibilities, and applications of Electronics and Computer Engineering Technology, Mechanical Engineering Technology, and Computer Programming. Students explore how engineering technology bridges theoretical concepts and practical applications through industry-relevant software, programming tools, and hands-on laboratory activities.

Duration: Four Weeks (Weeks 10-13)

Module Topics: Introduction to Engineering Technology Practices and Applications

  1. Introduction to Engineering Technology

    • Overview of engineering technology disciplines and career opportunities.
    • Roles and responsibilities of electronics and computer engineering technologists.
    • Introduction to Mechanical Engineering Technology and its applications.
    • Engineering technology as a bridge between theory and practice.

  2. Introduction to Engineering Programming

    • Programming fundamentals using Python and MATLAB.
    • Applications of programming in engineering problem-solving and data analysis.
    • Introduction to engineering software tools and computational techniques.

  3. Engineering Software and Practical Applications

    • Computer and electronics engineering technology practices.
    • Electronics and computer engineering software applications.
    • Mechanical engineering and architectural design software.
    • Practical applications of software tools in engineering design, analysis, and problem-solving.
