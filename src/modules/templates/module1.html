Module 1: Application of AI in the Field of Engineering Technology

This module introduces the history, philosophy, and methodologies of the engineering and technology professions, with a focus on computer, electrical, and mechanical disciplines. It also provides first-semester students with essential strategies and resources to help them succeed academically in their engineering technology studies

Duration: four weeks (Weeks 1-4)

Module Topics: Introduction to Engineering and Technology

  1. History, Philosophy, and Achievements in Engineering and Technology

    • Major achievements in engineering and technology
    • Introduction to computer, electrical, and mechanical engineering technology
    • The role and impact of engineering and technology in society

  2. Essential Skills and Potentials for Success in Engineering Technology

    • Academic success strategies for first-semester students
    • Essential mathematical, analytical, problem-solving, and technical skills
    • Professional competencies and lifelong learning

  3. Teaching and Learning Processes in Engineering Technology

    • Engineering technology learning methods and hands-on experimentation
    • Problem-based learning, collaboration, and technical communication
    • Exploring key topics across engineering technology disciplines

  4. Introduction to Generative AI in Engineering and Technology

    • Understanding generative AI (GenAI) and its applications
    • AI-assisted learning, research, and engineering problem-solving
    • Ethical use of AI, critical evaluation of AI-generated information, and responsible decision-making

  5. Engineering Technology Topics and Global Collaboration

    • Interdisciplinary engineering challenges and real-world applications
    • Global engineering collaboration and teamwork
    • Cross-cultural communication, collaborative problem-solving, and AI-supported teamwork
